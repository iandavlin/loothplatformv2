## The three decisions

### 1. Timezone: UTC — and the old reader was FOUR HOURS LATE
Two independent proofs. The old plugin's own source
(`cutover/batch-output/BATCH-04-results.md:158`): *"stored as `Y-m-d H:i:s` UTC"*. And
the data agrees without it — `user_registered` is UTC, user 1829 registered
`2026-04-21 21:11:27` with expiry `2026-07-28 21:11:00`, **the same minute-of-day**;
user 1865 registered `15:26:04`, expiry `15:25:00`. Two for two.

⚠️ **Both boxes run `timezone_string = America/New_York`**, so `CompStanding`'s
site-zone read (left deliberately unsettled by #181) placed every expiry four hours
late. Harmless while nothing enforced. Not harmless now. Gate 89 asserts this against a
**hostile process timezone** set to America/New_York, so a reader that drops its
explicit UTC zone reddens.

### 2. An expired comp becomes whatever their sources already say
Not a flat looth1. The role comes off and `Arbiter::sync` re-arbitrates normally:

- also pays on **Patreon** → **looth3**, their real tier (gate 89 D2, F3)
- also pays on **Stripe** → their Stripe tier (D6)
- **no paying source anywhere** → **looth1**, the starter tier (D5) — never no tier at
  all, which is a broken account rather than a lapsed comp, and is also what the old
  plugin documented
- `payment_source=stripe` with **no** source row → **HELD, never demoted** (D7). A
  payer is not flattened because our data is ambiguous.

### 3. The two overdue accounts are held by a DATE, not a list
`effective_from`: only a timer that ran out **at or after** it is ever enforced.
Chosen over a skip-list because it cannot be defeated by a mistyped id and it holds
**every** already-overdue account on **every** box, including any nobody enumerated —
the same shape as the Arbiter's own `registeredAfterCutover`. Both held accounts are
surfaced in the tab and in every sweep's log, never silently reconciled.

## What was built

- **`platform/config/comp-expiry.php`** — tracked, `__DIR__`, with a gitignored
  `.local.php` overlay. `enabled` default **false**, `effective_from` default **empty**.
  Both fences fail closed, so `enabled => true` with an empty cutover is a real
  detect-and-report mode **with no third knob**. Not an env var: the sweep runs in the
  5-minute WP-Cron tick and lg-wp-cron.service carries no `Environment=`.
- **`Membership/CompExpiry.php`** — the policy and the sweep. Decides WHO has lapsed.
  ⚠️ Contains no `add_role`/`remove_role`/`wp_capabilities`/`set_role` at all, asserted.
- **`Arbiter.php`** — the looth4 early-return now asks whether the comp is still
  running. **Still the only writer of `wp_capabilities`.**
- **`Tick.php`** — pass 4, own try/catch.
- **`Admin.php`** — the **Comp Timers** tab. Sets and clears a date, writes the meta and
  never a role, parses input through the same `CompStanding::parse` that reads it back,
  normalises to `Y-m-d H:i:s` UTC, and says UTC on the field (both boxes run New York).

### Two placement details in `Arbiter::sync` that are load-bearing
1. `$oldTier` is captured as `looth4` **before** the role comes off, or
   `looth_tier_changed` fires with the wrong `from` and profile-app purges against a
   tier the member never held.
2. The stripe coexistence guard gained `! $compExpired`. A comp whose role was just
   removed holds no tier for an instant, so `empty(intersect looth1)` is TRUE for them
   and that guard would return early leaving them with **no looth role whatsoever** —
   worse than any demotion. The genuinely ambiguous case is held *above*, before the
   role comes off.
