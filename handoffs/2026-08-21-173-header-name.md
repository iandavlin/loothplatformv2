## ⚠️ THE ROW HAS TWO MORE STACKING THINGS IN IAN'S BAND — measured, and only one of them is fixed

Ian's words were *"we are stacking words that used to be inline"*, and three
separate things stack in that band. Measured with his own name, both variants,
counting rendered line boxes:

| what stacks | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| the account name | 2 lines at 1100/1024 (pill on) | **1 line at every width** |
| the `Looth Group` wordmark | **2 lines** at 1100/1024 (pill on) | **1 line** |
| the nav's two-word items (`The Hub`, `The Map`) | 2 lines from 1154 down (pill on); from 1100 down (pill off) | **pill off: fixed** — inline at 1100/1024/1010. **Pill on: UNCHANGED, still stacked from 1154 down** |

So for every member **without** the tester pill — which is everyone but the
cohort — the whole row is now inline down to 1010. **With the pill on, the two
two-word nav links still stack below ~1154, exactly as they do on main.**

**Why it was not "just fixed too."** The nav sits at its `min-width:auto`
min-content of 419.2px in that band, and `white-space: nowrap` on
`.lg-chrome__menu a` would raise that floor to roughly 460 — turning the stack
into a **horizontal overflow** of about 40px instead. That is the identical
trade the flat-`max-width` mutation demonstrates (gate 87 M3): with the pill
present the row has no slack left to spend, so a fix inside it can only move the
damage, not remove it.

**It belongs with the 821–885px finding, not here** — same root cause (#170's
pill costs the row ~76px), same conclusion (nothing inside the name or the nav
can buy the space back), and it should be decided once, for both. What actually
frees room is one of: hiding the wordmark earlier, collapsing the nav to the
hamburger earlier than 820, or shortening `The Hub`/`The Map`. All three are
Ian's calls, not a lane's.
