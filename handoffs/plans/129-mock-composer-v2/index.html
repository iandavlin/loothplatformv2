Composer v2 — write first, categorize last

Mock for #129 (ledger 44), approved plan v2. The required Where step leaves the composer; new discussions land in General; an optional topic step arrives at the end, encouragement-framed. A picked topic maps topic → forum behind the scenes.

Look at §2, Option C — that is Ian's 8/19 ruling: add-a-tag plus a modal with a proper hierarchical layout. Options A and B are the two shapes drawn before that ruling; they are kept below only so the reasoning is on the record, and both are superseded.

Nothing here is wired. Static HTML, no app CSS or JS loaded — the frames use the real class names and the real token values so the colours and metrics match the running composer.
Chips are real data: every term shown is an actual shared_category ("Content Topics") term measured on dev2, with its real use count. The forum a chip lands on is the measured mapping.
Desktop is the 4-step wizard; mobile is the flat form. That split is real — buildNtmWizard() returns null below 641px, so mobile never gets a wizard. The chip strip therefore has to work in both shapes, and both are drawn.

1 · What leaves removed

The step that was first and required becomes no step at all. Today a member must choose one of 37 postable forums from a colour-coded accordion before they may type a title. Ian, 8/16: the Where step is "a pain point for posting".

2 · Option C — add-a-tag, then a hierarchical picker Ian's ruling 8/19

Ian, 8/19 (verbatim): "I see it more being add in the tags and maybe popping up a new modal with a decent heirarchical layout." So: the end-of-flow placement stays, but the inline affordance is a tag field — chips accumulate as tags, each one removable — and ＋ Add topics opens a picker that presents the taxonomy as a hierarchy, parents with their children, rather than the flat chip cloud Options A and B drew.

The hierarchy is the layout, not a label on it. On desktop the parents are a list on the left and the selected parent's children fill the right pane — 7 parents, 29 children, each with the number of posts already using it, so a member can see which topics are live. On mobile the same tree is an accordion in a bottom sheet. Both carry a search box, because 36 terms is past scanning, and both let you tag the parent itself ("Tag the whole area") — which is what makes ruling (b) below matter, since two of those parents have no forum to land in.

4 · Option B — folded into Review superseded 8/19

No third step: the flow is Write → Photos → Review and the chip strip is a block on the Review page, just above Post. Shortest possible flow — one fewer screen than today, and the chips sit at the literal last moment. The cost is that the strip has to compete with the summary rows for attention, so fewer people will notice it, which works against the "bonus points" nudge. Mobile is barely different from Option A, since mobile is one scroll either way.

Superseded by Ian's 8/19 ruling. Kept on the record as the shortest-flow variant, and because its "Forum" review row is the affordance an editing member needs once the Where step is gone — that part is still in the plan.

5 · Two things the mock can't decide for you

Both came out of the Phase-1 measurement and are on #129 as a comment. They change what the Lands in line says, not how the step looks — so this mock stands either way, but the code can't be written until they're settled.

  • Repair and Restoration
  • New Builds
  • Tools, Spaces, Robots and Widgets
  • Business
  • Vintage
  • Perspective
(a) "General" is a child of "Repair and Restoration", not a site-wide forum. So defaulting every untagged discussion there files it inside the repair tree — a member asking a business question with no chip lands under Repair and Restoration. Accept it, make a genuinely top-level General, or land the default in the parentless block?

(b) Two of the four big chips have nowhere natural to land. Repair and Restoration has its General and Business has General Business, but New Builds (145 uses) and Tools, Spaces, Robots and Widgets (97 uses) have no generic child at all. Create General sub-forums under each, map to the busiest child (Acoustic Builds / Tools and Jigs — both a guess), or let them fall to the default and inherit (a)?

Together these two are 25.5% of all term use, so not an edge case. In these frames the chips shown for Tools resolve to a real leaf (3D Printing), which is why the mapping reads cleanly here — pick the parent chip instead and (b) is what decides the answer.
