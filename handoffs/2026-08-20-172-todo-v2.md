## The TEST-URL convention — NEW, and keeper owns it from here

**It did not exist.** Grepped every branch and every commit body on main before
building: zero hits. So this lane defined it, in one comment above `build_todo`
in `tools/lanes-page.py`, and it is documented in `docs/domains/PAGE.md`.

A record is one line a lane or keeper already writes:

    TEST-URL: /lgjoin/            TEST-URL #148: /lgjoin/
    ACTION #148: Look at the join page — three tiers and their prices

Read first-hit-wins from: the issue's **comments** then its **body** (live — a
correction lands on the next 5-minute redraw with nothing to merge) → **commit
bodies on main** (deliberately *not* `--first-parent`, so a lane can write the
record at build time and not only keeper at merge) → the **park reason**.

**⚠ KEEPER: this is the thing to carry forward.** One `TEST-URL:` line in a
merge body and that bullet gets a working door, with no page edit ever. An
`ACTION:` line beside it and the bullet gets Ian's own words instead of a verb
plus a title.

## Two defects found by the work, not by a fixture

1. **The list led with a sentence nobody said.** `main()` upgrades a seat to
   *needs-you* when its issue wears `merged`/`built` and writes the reason
   itself; the checklist's first family — which exists for *a lane that raised
   its hand and named him* — could not tell that from a real hand-raise. It
   printed `Answer 138-phase-b — it asked: "merged — waiting on your check"`.
   Two defects from one line: a sentence attributed to a lane that never said
   it, **and** the issue was swallowed before the merged/built families ran, so
   #138 — the single item Ian's ruling sends to the quiet line — never reached
   it. The upgrade is now tagged at its source; **the seat card is unchanged**
   (that state and reason are #159's design), only the checklist looks past it.

2. **A park reason is ONE line**, so the line-start rule made that whole source
   unusable — a lane writes *"merged as c879589, dev2 flag ON pending Ian's
   join-page look"* and has nowhere to put a record. The record may now ride at
   the **end** of a park-reason line, and there only. The two rules differ
   because their sources differ, not for convenience. **Gate 77 found this**, not
   a reader.
