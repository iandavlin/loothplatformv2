Ruling 2 — what was actually wrong with the preview

Three mismatches at once, which is why it read as a detached card with an odd gap rather than as a slightly-off box:

 WasNow
Frame width640px624px — the email's own content width, so it fills edge to edge
Frame backgroundwhite#e8e2d8 — the email's own body colour, so no band can show around it
Elevationheavy drop shadow, on a dark sectionno shadow, on the light page, with a labelled bar anchoring it

The email's outer table is 960px max-width with a 624px content column and a #e8e2d8 body — so a 640px white frame was guaranteed to show a mismatched strip down both sides. It was a measurable cause, not a matter of taste.

Rulings 3+4 — and I have dropped my framing entirely

You were right that it was our invention. I had built up a “non-member carve-out” that made the rule about which audience a section governs. It is gone from this page and from the design docs — not softened, removed.

Your model is the one on the page now: the email announces this week's public content to everyone on the list; that content later goes members-only; so the email is how you catch it while it's open. That also answers the thing I was stuck on without needing a special case — non-members get the email because the email is the announcement, not because of an exception carved out for them.
