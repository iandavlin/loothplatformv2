The signup page’s email box — why it pans sideways, and what I want to change it to

Same live email in all three. Only the width of the box around it changes.

The one thing to check: In B you should be able to read every headline without dragging sideways. If B reads cleanly and C (your phone’s width) also reads cleanly, that’s the fix.
A — ON DEV2 NOW
Box is max-width:624px. The email’s own layout wants 960px + 32px of padding.
This is the box you saw. Try to read a long headline without dragging.
B — WHAT I RECOMMEND
Box sized to the email’s own document width: 992px (its 960px container + its 32px outer padding).
Nothing about the email changes — it is simply given the width it was built for. The “Scroll inside the message” caption gets deleted, because there is nothing left to scroll to.
C — YOUR PHONE
Box pinned to 390px, an iPhone’s width. The email has its own phone layout at 480px and under.
This is the panel I most want your eyes on. It is the one I could not test properly myself.

The alternative I did not pick, and why. I could shrink the whole email to fit the small box (a scale-to-fit). It never pans — but every headline gets smaller than the page around it, and on a phone it would be unreadably small. Giving the email its real width costs nothing and keeps the type at full size, so I would only go the other way if you want the box kept deliberately narrow.

Straight about what I have not proven. I measured the widths out of the email’s own code — a 960px container inside 32px of padding. I have not loaded this in a browser and measured what actually overflows, so panel A is the evidence, not my explanation of it. If A pans and B does not, the fix is right regardless of my reasoning.
