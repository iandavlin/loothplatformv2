Which notifications get this — and which do not

“Tapping a notification opens a reply modal” is right for the rows that are about a reply. The other three rows have nothing to reply to, so they keep doing exactly what they do today. This is a recommendation, not a decision I have made — say the word and I change it.

Gets the reply modal — 4 rows

“Dave replied to your post”, “replied to your comment”, “mentioned you in a discussion”, and “replied in a discussion you follow”.

The rule is quote whatever the notification points at: the reply if it rang you about one, and otherwise the discussion’s opening post — which is the case when someone @mentions you in a brand-new discussion, where there is no reply yet to quote.

Keeps today’s behaviour — 3 rows

Reactions (“Marla reacted to your post”) — there is nothing they wrote to reply to. Tapping still opens the discussion.

Messages go to the DM thread; connection rows go to the profile. Both already have their own reply surface and neither is a forum reply.

“Alice and 1 other replied”

When two people reply before you look, the row merges into one. The link already points at the newest reply, so that is the one the modal quotes.

Not hypothetical — I counted it on live: 3 of the 17 reply notifications there are merged right now, and one of them is four different people.

Recommendation: the modal says so in words — “showing the most recent of 4 replies” — rather than quietly showing one and hiding three.

Your half-written draft is safe

The composer already keeps a draft per discussion (your ruling). Opening this modal restores your own unfinished reply for that discussion, if you had one.

It never pre-fills with the other person’s words — that was the bug edit-post-parity fixed today, and I am reusing their composer precisely so it cannot come back.

Two things I found that you should know before picking

1. Option B needs a fetch that A does not. A quotes the reply that rang you. B also needs your original post, which is a second thing to load and a second thing that can fail on a bad connection. It is not a reason to reject B — it is why A is the recommendation.

2. Nothing today can hand me a reply’s text. The notification stores who and where, not what was said, and the only endpoint that reads a single reply returns its photos and nothing else. So either option needs a small new read — one reply, by id, reusing the same public-forum check the existing thread list already enforces. No new way to post a reply is being built: Post goes through the same endpoint the composer already uses.

3. One row in four cannot quote a reply, and I only found that by reading the code. An @mention in a brand-new discussion rings you before any reply exists, so there is nothing to quote but the opening post. Both options handle it the same way and neither breaks — I am flagging it because “show the reply that generated the notification” quietly has an exception, and you should hear it from me now rather than find it on your phone.

What is deliberately not changing

No second action on the row

Per your refinement: no two-action row. The row stays one tap, the modal is what that tap does, and the full-post link lives inside the modal.

thread-follow’s ⋯ menu stays

The per-row ⋯ (notify / email for that discussion) is already shipped and is untouched. Swipe-to-delete and the × are untouched too.

It ships switched off

This merges behind a flag that is off by default, so it reaches the dev server harmlessly and you look at the real running thing before anyone else can. Nothing goes live on a green test alone.
