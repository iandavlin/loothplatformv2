One thing I want to flag before building it

The Loothprint form is long, and you already think this modal is cramped. That is your own open complaint — “resizable, with text scaling” (backlog 10). Now measured rather than guessed: inside the dialog the form is 1,630px on a laptop and about 1,800px on a phone, so the dialog scrolls. The five needed things are above the fold and the four extras stay folded, and I think it holds — but this is the one place it could feel worse than the full-page version, and it is the thing to judge when you open it. If it does feel cramped, the fix is the one backlog 10 already asks for.

What it is made of

The toggle asks the server for the other form and swaps it in — the Loothprint half is the route that was already proven end to end, rendered without its page furniture. Nothing about the form, its saving or its photo uploads changed; only where it is shown.
