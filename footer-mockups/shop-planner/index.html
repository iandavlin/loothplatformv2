What's the same in both

  • The URL doesn't change. /shop-layout-planner/ keeps answering 200 throughout — I'll prove it before and after, and gate it so a future change can't quietly break it.
  • Every word of copy survives, including the FAQ and the sponsor block. That copy is what Google indexes and I'm not touching it.

  • Page 63845 (shop-planner-page) is left completely alone. It's members-gated, gets no public traffic, and isn't part of this.

What I'd book separately

  • Touch support for the canvas. This is the real gap. Today a phone visitor can't use the tool at all, and both mocks above just tell them so honestly rather than pretending. Adding touch drag + a mobile editing panel is a proper piece of work, not a tweak.
  • jsPDF is loaded from a third-party CDN (cdnjs) rather than served by us. PDF export is advertised on this page and currently depends on someone else's uptime. Worth vendoring.
