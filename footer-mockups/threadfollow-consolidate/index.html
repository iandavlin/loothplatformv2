The action row, consolidated — two ways

Your call: put notifications, emails, email frequency and Save behind one control that opens a small modal. Like, replies and Share stay inline. Both variants are live below — click the control on either card and the real modal opens.

One thing I did not do, on purpose. The consolidated control is not a ⋯ menu. Burying follow in ⋯ was tried and ruled the wrong surface twice (SPEC §2.3), and your original ruling was that the affordance must be visible, not buried. So the control is a labelled bell that carries its own state: it lights orange when you're following, and a small ✉ badge appears when emails are on. You can still read the state of a thread from the feed without opening anything — which is the property consolidation would otherwise cost you.

Why I'd pick B. The two things being merged are not the same kind of control. Save is a reflex you hit while scrolling — a "keep this" — and people do it dozens of times. Follow and email are a decision you make once per thread and then forget. Putting a frequent action behind a modal to keep company with two rare ones costs a tap every time to save a little width once. B also degrades better: if the row ever gets tight again, the bell is already icon-only and Save can drop its label without losing meaning.

Where A wins: it is the only version that gets the row down to four controls, and every state control lives in exactly one place, which is easier to explain and easier to keep consistent across card, modal and sheet. If you expect the row to grow again, A has the headroom.

Before you settle the frequency control — one finding

Frequency probably shouldn't be per-discussion. I went looking for what would send these digests and the answer changes the control, so it's worth your ruling before this gets built.

A digest exists to batch across threads — one email instead of nine. But this control sets a cadence per discussion. Follow six threads on Daily and you have signed up for six separate daily emails, which is the thing the member was trying to escape. Set thread A to Hourly and thread B to Daily and there is no sensible way to combine them, so they can never share an email.

Recommendation: make the cadence an account-level setting — "email me about discussions I follow: Instant / Hourly / Daily / Weekly" — and let this modal show it rather than own it, e.g. Emails · on — Daily (change). The per-discussion decision stays a clean on/off, which is what §0 ruling 1 already made it, and one member gets one digest containing every thread they follow.

It also collapses the storage problem: one preference per member instead of one per (member × thread), and it fits the account-level email prefs that already exist (§6).

What this needs from elsewhere

There is no existing sender that can do this today, and I want to be straight about that rather than let a control ship that quietly does nothing. lg-weekly-digest is an editorial newsletter — one issue composed by hand, sent to a CRM tag on a weekly cron. It has no notion of "who follows thread X", so it cannot host per-thread reply batching; reusing it would be the wrong mechanism. Per-member reply digests are closer to what notify-bridge already does for the bell (it coalesces to one row per thread) and would need a per-member queue plus a sweep to flush it.

So: Instant works today — that's the existing per-reply email path. Hourly, Daily and Weekly need infrastructure that does not exist yet, and that is weekly-recap's side. Raised with them on the board.
