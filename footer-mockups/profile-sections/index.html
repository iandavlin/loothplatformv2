Profile — where should the Sections picker live?

Ian: “I don’t currently like the way the section picker is deployed on mobile. It’s a weird button in the privacy controls.” Confirmed. Here is what it is today, a recommendation, and one alternative. Nothing has been changed.

Drawn from the running system on dev2 at repo 6ef25e3. Frames are illustrative, not pixel-exact. The picker is a drawer on every width below 1380px — phones, tablets and most laptops. At ≥1380px it is a permanent sidebar and the button is display:none; both proposals leave that desktop behaviour untouched.

Today as built

The only way to open the picker is an amber pill in the top-right of the dark Profile controls panel — the panel that otherwise holds only privacy settings.

Why A is the recommendation

  • It fixes the actual complaint. “Sections” stops being a member of the privacy group. The dark panel goes back to meaning one thing: who can see what.
  • It is reachable after scrolling. Today the only opener is pinned to the top of the page; once a member scrolls down to their blocks, adding a section means scrolling all the way back up. The end-of-list card puts the control where the task ends.
  • It matches the mental model already in the drawer. The picker’s own hint says “drag a section into your profile — or tap to add”. A dashed slot at the end of the list is the drop target, drawn.
  • It adds no new floating layer. Both new affordances sit in normal document flow.
  • Desktop is untouched. At ≥1380px the permanent sidebar still wins; the new row and card are the <1380px story only.

Why not B

  • The bottom of a mobile screen is already taken — bottom-nav.js puts a fixed 54px tab bar (plus safe-area) on /u/, with a raised centre Post button. A second docked bar stacks on it and competes with that button.
  • It costs vertical height on every screen, permanently, for a control most members use rarely — a member sets their layout up once and then mostly edits content.
  • It is still a floating bar rather than something anchored to the thing it affects.
One thing to decide either way. The Sections button today disappears whenever you switch View as to Public or Member — the whole editor is not rendered in preview mode (u.php:127). So the entry point is not just oddly placed, it is intermittent. Worth confirming that is intended before either proposal is built.
