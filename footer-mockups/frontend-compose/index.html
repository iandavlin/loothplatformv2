Why I’d still pick the single screen

It is five fields. A wizard earns its clicks when a form is long enough to frighten someone; this one fits on a phone screen with room to spare once the extras are folded. Three steps here mostly adds two taps and hides how nearly-done you are.

And the wizard we have is not reusable. The discussion one is desktop-only — below 641px it switches itself off and serves the flat form, so your members on phones are already using the single-screen shape. It is also hand-wired to six specific fields of the discussion form, so pointing it at loothprints means writing a second one, not configuring this one.

What this is actually made of

Worth knowing before you pick, because it is why this is small:

The front doormissingThis is the build.

Loothprints and events work this way. Videos, articles and sponsor posts do not — their pages are laid out by hand, so a form alone would give you a post with no page. That is a separate, bigger job and I would not fold it into this one.

One thing I need you to decide

Who gets this form, and does what they post go live straight away?

GateWho could postVerdict
The obvious one WordPress gives us~1,850 members — essentially everyone signed upToo broad for videos and articles
Open for loothprints, allow-list for the rest (what I’d build — it’s what your backlog already asks for)Any member for loothprints; named people for the restMatches how you already run it

My default, unless you say otherwise: a member’s loothprint publishes straight away — they already submit these — and anything on the gated types from someone not on the list lands as pending for you rather than going live. So even a mistake in the gate is a queue, not a page on the site.

What I need from you

Single screen or wizard, and loothprint first — or name a different type. Then I build it behind a flag, off by default, so it reaches the box harmlessly and you click the real form before anyone else can see it.
