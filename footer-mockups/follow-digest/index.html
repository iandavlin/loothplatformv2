Follow digest — what Daily and Weekly actually send

The frequency control has been built and deliberately hidden since 2026-07-30, because storing a cadence does nothing without a sender. This is the sender. Everything below is drawn from real live data — the busy day is Hide Glue Working Time, 8 June 2026, and the names are the people who actually replied. Nothing here is invented.

1The argument for the feature, in one picture

One real day, one real discussion, one member following it. On the left is what arrives today. On the right is what Daily sends instead. Same information, same deep links, same day.

This is the case that justifies the build. Over the trailing 30 days only 8 members would have received anything at all, and the heaviest got 2 items in a month — on that window alone Daily batches nothing and is a strictly worse Instant. But over a year, this thread produced 15 replies in a single day. The feature earns its keep in the tail, not the mean.
Counts and names only — never reply text. That is the standing privacy ruling (NOTIF-EMAIL-STATE §1), not a style choice. The digest tells you who is talking and links you to the thread; it never quotes anybody into your inbox.
Why 14 and not 15. That day had 15 replies, but the member reading this is Murray, who wrote one of them. Your own reply never appears in your own digest — so the count is 14 from 5 people, not 15 from 6. It is the oldest bug in every digest ever written, and it is a gate assertion rather than a good intention.

2The case members will actually get: one reply

The busy day above is the tail. The normal case is a single reply in a single thread — so the template has to read correctly at n=1 first and scale up, not the reverse. An email that says "3 new replies across 2 discussions" reads absurd when it is almost always one.

3The control — and the word that keeps it honest

You ruled that cadence is one account-level setting, not per-thread. The control still lives in the follow modal where members already are — but it has to say so, or it reads as "Daily for this discussion" and quietly changes every other one.

Why per-thread cadence was declined and should stay declined: a digest exists to batch across threads. Set it per discussion and following six threads on Daily is six daily emails — precisely what the member was trying to escape.
Dimmed and inert, not just dimmed. Dimming alone still accepts the click, which would be a setting that isn't — the same lie in miniature. With Emails off there is no cadence to pick.

4What this costs to get wrong — the one line I am most afraid of

A member turning Daily on for the first time has no watermark. Read naively that is the beginning of time, and their first digest is the entire reply history of every thread they follow. Email cannot be recalled.

The rule, and it is gated red-first: set the watermark to now the moment cadence is first written. A digest is never a backfill. One account on live holds 335 subscriptions — for them, the naive read is thousands of items about conversations they finished months ago.

Ships behind a flag, defaulted OFF, and the OFF state is provable rather than asserted — it is the identity function on one filter, and no scheduled sender is registered at all. The gate asserts the absence: no cron event, no cadence field, no control in the DOM. That missing absence-assertion is the blind spot behind six of the misses on this platform.
