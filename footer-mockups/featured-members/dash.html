Two things for you to rule on, Ian.
  • One at a time, or several? — RULED 12 Aug: one at a time. No rotation. If you want a rotation (say three, cycling daily) the dash grows a running order and the band picks one per page load — say so now, it is much cheaper before the build than after.
  • Should the dash be able to ask? Drawn deliberately with no way to add someone who has not ticked the box — no override, not even for you. If you want a “nudge this member to opt in” button that emails them, that is a separate build and needs its own ruling.
