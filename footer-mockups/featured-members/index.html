What we need from you

Everything else we can decide ourselves. These eight change what gets built.

  1. Tickbox: A or B? A row in the privacy bar the member already has (recommended), or its own card underneath it. Drawn side by side →
  2. The consent wording. “Include me as a possible featured member — the Looth Group may show my photo, name and what I do on the front page.” That sentence is the whole agreement. Change any of it.
  3. Untick while featured. Off the front page immediately (recommended), or stays up until you swap them out.
  4. One at a time, or a rotation? Drawn as one, because that is what the band renders today. A rotation is cheap now and expensive later — and the pool is not the constraint: 1,477 live members already have a photo and a line, so there is no shortage to rotate through.
  5. Live or frozen? Should the card follow their profile as they edit it (recommended), or keep the details as they were when you picked them?
  6. Can the dash ask people? Drawn with no override and no invitations — the only way into the pool is a member ticking their own box. A “nudge them to opt in” email would be a separate build.
  7. What the button says. “See Beau’s profile”, or something else.
  8. Should the tickbox also ask for a line? Measured on live: 1,477 members could make a good card, but only 6 have a public About section — so nearly every card will be photo + name + one line + location, with no paragraph. An optional “anything you’d like us to say about you?” box beside the tickbox is the only way most cards ever get a sentence. See what the normal card looks like →
How it would ship. The tickbox is member-facing, so it merges behind a flag defaulted off: it reaches the dev2 serve as a proven no-op, you look at the real running thing, and only then does it get switched on. The dash is admin-only and the front-page band already exists. Nobody appears on the front page at any point in that sequence without having ticked the box first.
