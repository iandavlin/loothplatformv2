The location line is already handled — and it needs one more turn of the screw. The band today hides where from logged-out visitors, and that rule stays. But a real member also has their own location settings (“city”, “state only”, “private”, set separately for members and for the public), and the card must honour those too — not just “is this a member”. A member whose location is private shows no location line to anybody.

Two things for you to rule on, Ian.
  • Live or frozen? Drawn as live — the card reads their profile on every page load, so fixing a typo on their profile fixes the front page. The alternative is to copy their details at the moment you pick them, so the card cannot change under you. Recommend live: it is the whole point of “automatically added”.
  • What the button says. Drawn as See Beau’s profile. Could be Visit their shop where a member has a business link, or stay one fixed phrase for everyone.
