Your card would be a photo and a name. Featured members get a card with their photo, their name, one line about what they do, and where they are. You are missing the line about what you do — add it and your card is ready.

Recommendation: the meter nudges, it does not refuse. A member who wants to volunteer is allowed to volunteer — we just tell them, plainly, what their card would look like right now. Blocking consent behind a percentage would be a strange thing to do to someone who is trying to say yes, and it would make the pool look empty for reasons nobody can see.

Two different measures, on purpose. Profile % is the member’s own progress, all eight items. Card status is only about the four things the front-page card actually shows. A member can be at 62% and make a perfect card; a member at 37% can be missing exactly the one line that matters. Feature is only offered on a ready card — that is the one hard rule, and it protects the front page rather than the member.

Read that top bar again. 978 members — 56% of everyone — are at 12%: a photo and nothing else. Another 553 are at 25%. So 88% of the membership is at a quarter-filled or worse, and only 29 members are above 60%. Individual pieces: 98% have a photo, 41% have set a location, 9% have added a single link, and under 5% have written one line about what they do.
  • Why this happened: a photo and a name came across automatically when profiles were created. Everything else has to be typed by a member who was never asked to.
  • What it means for featuring: only 66 members could make a complete card today. The pool is not limited by who consents — it is limited by who has filled anything in.
