One thing I did NOT change, because it is your call and not a dark-mode fix. On the light theme, the lit “Following” label measures 3.45 : 1 against its own tint — below the 4.5 accessibility threshold for text that size. That is the brand orange itself, which we deliberately made ONE colour across all five follow surfaces. Fixing it properly means changing that orange everywhere, so I have left it alone and flagged the number rather than quietly altering a brand colour inside a dark-mode pass. Dark uses the lighter orange the rest of the dark theme already uses, and is fine.

Everything here is behind the off-by-default flag; it is on for this box only, never for live.
