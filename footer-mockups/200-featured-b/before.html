Welcome

Built by guitar looths, for guitar looths.

The Looth Group is where serious luthiers and builders share tools, techniques, and the candid conversations you cannot find on YouTube. It is a working community — not a marketing channel.

  • The full archive of build threads, repair tutorials, and shop-tested fixes.
  • Member video library — long-form workshops from working looths.
  • Downloads: plans, jigs, templates, and reference files.
  • Active discussion forum where questions actually get answered.
