One thing I found by clicking it, and fixed.

The drawing had the Put me forward button going nowhere — in a mock a button never has to work, so nobody had pressed it. On the real page it was pointing at an address that gives a “not found” page. It now takes you to your own profile, which is where the tick box lives. Signed out, it asks you to sign in first and then brings you straight back. A dead button on the front page would have been worse than the empty space it replaced.

What the card does now

  • When nobody is picked, the front page says This spot is open and invites members to put themselves forward — instead of leaving a hole, or re-running someone from June.
  • The button goes to the member’s own profile, where “include me as a possible featured member” is ticked.
  • The old hand-placed card has not been thrown away. It is still there and still tested, so going back to it is a one-word change if you ever want it.
  • Nothing about your picks changed. When you have picked somebody, they show, exactly as before.
