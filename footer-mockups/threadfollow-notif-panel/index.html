Thread-follow — previs v2

Per discussion, two independent opt-ins: notifications and emails. Both default OFF for everyone, always. Nothing subscribes you — not creating a topic, not replying, not being @mentioned. You can hold either, both, or neither.

Spec: docs/atlas/THREAD-FOLLOW-SPEC.md · branch threadfollow-spec · cites main@c10df43 · spec + mock only, nothing built

What you're gating

  1. Set them on the feed card and in the open discussion modal, in the header cluster beside the S/M/L/XL size control.
  2. Unset them from three places, all reaching the same store — the thread itself, the notifications-panel row ⋯ menu, and an unsubscribe link in the email.
  3. The email link works logged out, is specific to that discussion, and lands on a confirmation page that also offers "stop all discussion emails" for someone who is really done.

Frame 1 + 2 — the feed card

Two toggles on the carddecide this one

The card's action row already carries six controls; two more makes eight. Cards A and B show the two-icon proposal (on/off states); the third card shows the fallback — one labelled control that expands two switches. Both are visible affordances, neither is buried in the ⋯ menu. If the two-icon row reads as cluttered to you, we take the fallback.

Frame 3 — the discussion modal

Beside the size control

The cluster reads [title] [bell] [mail] [M] [×] — the two state controls grouped together, close rightmost. Second block shows both OFF, which is what every discussion looks like until someone clicks. Third block is the mobile sheet header, which has no S/M/L/XL control at all — so its placement is a question for you.

Frame 4 — the email unsubscribe page

One click, no login, this discussion only

Reached from a signed link in the email, so it works for someone logged out in their inbox. Stopping email for one discussion does not touch its notifications — the page says so. "Stop all discussion emails" is present but secondary. The link shows this page rather than acting immediately, because mail scanners follow links and would otherwise unsubscribe people who never clicked.

Live: mock-unsub.html · dark · after the click

Frame 5 — the notifications panel

The row ⋯ menu carries both switches

New replies in a discussion you follow arrive as one row that counts up, never one row per reply. The ⋯ on the row can switch either channel off — or on, since rows also reach people following nothing. Mention rows offer both plus "Remove my mention", which now unlinks your name and changes nothing else.

Two things we need from you — we have deliberately not decided these

1 · The 1,519 subscriptions that already exist. Live currently has 1,519 topic and 46 forum subscriptions, and they are emailing real members today — 31 discussion emails in the last fortnight. Opt-in-only is a rule for new follows; it says nothing about those. Measured: 72% of them were created by exactly the auto-subscribe you just banned (the subscriber authored or replied in that thread), but 93% sit on threads nobody has replied to in 90 days — so the people actually affected today are 49 members across 112 subscriptions. Five options are laid out in the spec (§9.2), from "leave them and just make them visible and one click from off" to "retire the dormant tail". We are not proposing to mass- unsubscribe anyone.

2 · Per-event email vs the weekly digest. An earlier ruling turned all per-event discussion email permanently off. The email you got on 7/26 and called wanted was a "new discussion" email from a forum subscription (46 of those) — a different thing from the "new reply" emails from topic subscriptions (1,519 of those) that fire on every reply. One switch was covering two different things. Options in the spec (§9.1); the two-toggle design leans toward "an explicit email toggle is what earns you the email," but that is your call.
