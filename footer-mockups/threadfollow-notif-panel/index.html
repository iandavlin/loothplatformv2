Thread-follow — previs v2

Per discussion, two independent opt-ins: notifications and emails. Both default OFF for everyone, always. Nothing subscribes you — not creating a topic, not replying, not being @mentioned. You can hold either, both, or neither.

Two icons, as you chose

Frame 2 — the mobile card needs you

Recommended: the same two icons, right end of the mobile bar

Mobile cards use a different, sparser action bar — only Like, replies and Share, with wide gaps — so unlike desktop there is room, with space to spare. The alternative is to leave mobile cards alone and let the sheet header carry both. Cleaner card, but then someone scrolling the feed can't opt in to anything without opening every thread first, which is most of what a card control is for. Both are drawn.

Frame 4 — the email unsubscribe page

One click, no login, this discussion only

Reached from a signed link in the email, so it works for someone logged out in their inbox. Stopping email for one discussion does not touch its notifications — the page says so. "Stop all discussion emails" is present but secondary. The link shows this page rather than acting immediately, because mail scanners follow links and would otherwise unsubscribe people who never clicked.

Frame 5 — the notifications panel

The row ⋯ menu carries both switches

New replies in a discussion you follow arrive as one row that counts up, never one row per reply. The ⋯ on the row can switch either channel off — or on, since rows also reach people following nothing. Mention rows offer both plus "Remove my mention", which now unlinks your name and changes nothing else.

Frame 6 — the account page needs you

Recommended: one new "Discussion emails" row

You already ship Weekly Digest and Event Reminders there. The rule that keeps them from contradicting each other: the account page is a master switch per kind of email; the per-discussion switches decide which discussions are in that kind. Master off means nothing is sent, whatever the individual discussions say — so the page can never claim "off" while mail is still arriving. It also shows a count, so a member can see how many discussions are emailing them.

The alternative — no master, and the account page lists every discussion instead — is drawn underneath. Honest, but someone following thirty threads gets a thirty-row settings page and no single "make it stop" button, which is the one thing people want when they open that page annoyed.

Frame 7 — the 1,519 already emailing members needs you

Recommended: change no data — make them visible and one tap from off

Live has 1,519 topic and 46 forum subscriptions sending real email today — 31 discussion emails in the last fortnight. 72% of them were created by exactly the auto-subscribe you banned (the person authored or replied in that thread). But 93% sit on threads nobody has replied to in 90 days, so the people actually affected today are 49 members across 112 subscriptions.

The frame shows the recommendation as a member would experience it rather than as a table: the email they already get grows a discussion-specific unsubscribe link, the card and thread finally show them they're subscribed, and the account page names the count with one switch that stops all of it. Deleting the 1,091 unasked-for ones is not recommended — that would be a second change made on their behalf, in the other direction, and it would take 428 apparently deliberate subscribes with it. The one alternative — also retire the 1,407 dormant ones — is drawn at the bottom with its cost.

One thing left that has no frame — the email posture

An earlier ruling turned all per-event discussion email permanently off, with the weekly digest as the only email surface. The email you got on 26 July and called wanted was a "new discussion" email from a forum subscription — 46 of those exist, and they fire only when someone starts a thread. That is a different thing from the "new reply" emails from topic subscriptions — 1,519 of those, firing on every reply. One switch was covering two very different things.

With opt-in now the rule, the natural answer is that an explicit envelope toggle is what earns you the email — a per-discussion email switch that never sends email would be a contradiction. But that is your call, not ours, so nothing has been decided.
