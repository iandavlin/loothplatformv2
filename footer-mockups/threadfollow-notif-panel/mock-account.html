Recommended — one new row. Account = master switch per email class; per-thread toggles = which threads are in that class.

You get emails from 3 discussions. See them in the Hub →

Changes save automatically.

The rule that stops this page ever lying: if this switch is off, no discussion email is sent — whatever the individual discussions say. Turn it back on and each discussion goes back to whatever you last set it to.

For a member: one obvious place to make it all stop, and the per-thread switches stay where the threads are. Nothing is hidden and nothing contradicts.

Alternative — no master; the account page lists every discussion you're emailed about.

For a member: honest, but unbounded — someone following thirty threads gets a thirty-row settings page, and there is no single “make it stop” button, which is the exact thing people look for when they are annoyed enough to open this page.

Weekly Digest stays independent either way, so a member who kills discussion email still gets the weekly recap — which is the whole point of having a recap.
