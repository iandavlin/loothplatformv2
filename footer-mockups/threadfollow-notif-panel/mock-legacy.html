Why not just delete them? 1,091 of these were created without consent — but removing them without asking would be a second change made on the member’s behalf, in the other direction, and 428 of them look like deliberate subscribes we would be throwing away with the rest. Making the state visible and one click from off is the honest remedy: the people who never wanted it can leave in one tap, from inside the very email that annoyed them, and the ones who did want it keep it. It also clears itself over time without us guessing.

The one alternative worth considering — also retire the dormant tail

Unsubscribe the 1,407 subscriptions on threads nobody has replied to in 90 days; keep the 112 live ones.

For a member: nobody currently receiving email is affected, because dormant threads send nothing. But if one of those threads wakes up years later, someone who genuinely subscribed stops hearing about it and will never know why.

The 12,948 group subscriptions (1,853 members) are a separate, larger population this spec has never covered — group-linked forums mail through a different branch. Flagged, untouched, and no decision here should be described as “covering the subscriptions” while that sits there.
