Backlog 19 · nothing is built

New members arrive as empty shells

You saw it in the directory: rows of identical grey faces. This page is the one decision that fixes it, with the picture under each option and a recommendation. Nothing below is built — the build starts when you have ruled.

First, the thing that changes the plan

There is no sign-up form to add a step to

The backlog line said “a sign-up step or a first-login prompt”. Having read the real code: a sign-up step cannot exist, because members never fill in a sign-up form. WordPress registration is switched off (users_can_register = 0) and the sign-ups table is empty. Whichever rail they come in on, the account is built for them — from what Patreon hands back, or from what they typed at checkout. Either way it is a name, an email and a tier, and nothing about who they are or what they do.

So the platform gets exactly one moment with a brand-new member's attention: the “you're in” screen at the end of joining. That moment already exists on both rails — and today we spend all of it on a password or a receipt.

Both rails, one experience. People join two ways right now and will keep doing so, so the new step hangs off becoming a member — not off anything Patreon-shaped or Stripe-shaped. A Patreon joiner and a Stripe joiner get the identical screen:

Where it hooks: on membership becoming active, which is the one thing both rails already agree on — they each record who a member is in the same place (lg_role_sources, with a source of patreon or stripe). The step is deliberately not wired to the Patreon callback or the Stripe webhook, so neither rail can end up with an experience the other one lacks — and a third rail later inherits it for free.

The decision

Ask them during the welcome, or nudge them afterwards?

Both options ask for the same three things — a photo, a city, and one line about what they do — and both are skippable. The difference is when we ask.

Option A — recommended

One more screen at the end of joining

However they joined, the last thing they see before landing is this — asked while they are still in the flow and still paying attention.

/welcome/profile  (same screen from either rail)

Nearly there, Marty.

Your profile is live right now — it just looks empty. Three quick answers and people can actually find you.

Start typing a town or city…
Acoustic repairs and neck resets
Skip — continue to my profile →

Drawn with the real welcome-page stylesheet, so this is how it would actually render.

  • Catches them at the only moment they are already stopped and answering things.
  • One screen serves both rails, so Patreon and Stripe joiners cannot drift apart.
  • Skipping already works and already goes to their profile.
  • One more screen between joining and getting in. Some will skip it.

Costs one extra screen. Everything it needs — the chrome, the skip path, the photo uploader, the city field — already exists somewhere in the platform, and both rails already have a “you're in” page to hand off from.

Option B — the alternative

Leave the welcome alone, nudge afterwards

They land on the site as they do today, and a card follows them around until the profile is filled in.

Add a photo, your city, and a line about what you do — that is all the directory needs to show you properly.

… the rest of the front page carries on below.

Uses the completeness meter the featured-members work already defines — same eight items.

  • Nothing stands between joining and getting into the community.
  • Keeps asking, so it can catch them on a later visit.
  • Asks at the moment they are least interested — they came to look around, not to do admin.
  • It is a banner. People are extremely good at ignoring banners.
  • Needs a new dismiss-and-remember rule, so it is more to build, not less.

Costs a new surface. And the front page is a bad host for it — it currently wipes its own web address of any extra information the moment it loads, which is exactly how a “you just joined” message would get lost.

Recommendation: Option A. We only get one moment of a new member's attention and we are currently spending all of it on a password or a receipt. Adding one screen to a flow they are already in is both the cheaper build and the one likely to actually get answers — and because it is skippable, nobody is trapped. It is also the option that keeps the two rails identical: one screen, reached from either, rather than a Patreon experience and a Stripe experience that quietly drift apart. Option A does not rule out Option B: the nudge is the natural follow-up later for the people who skip, and it reuses the same meter.

If you say nothing, nothing changes — the next member joins tomorrow with a grey face and no location, the same as the 33 before them.
